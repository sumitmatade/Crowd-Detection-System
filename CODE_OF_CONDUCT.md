# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussion about the work.

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.
